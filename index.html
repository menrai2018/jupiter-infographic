Atmospheric Composition

Jupiter is a true gas giant. Lacking a hard solid surface, its atmosphere seamlessly transitions into liquid interior layers under immense pressure.

Element Distribution

Hydrogen (H₂) 89.8%
Helium (He) 10.2%
Methane, Ammonia, Water Vapor & Dust < 0.1%
Ammonia crystal clouds in Jupiter's upper atmosphere create its characteristic orange and rust-colored horizontal cloud belts.

Outer Atmosphere

Composed mainly of hydrogen and helium with traces of methane and ammonia. Violent winds reach up to 620 km/h (385 mph), forging swirling cloud bands.

THE GREAT RED SPOT

Jupiter's iconic Great Red Spot is a colossal high-pressure storm larger than planet Earth itself. Spinning counterclockwise at ferocious speeds, this anticyclone has been continuously observed since at least 1831.

The Four Galilean Moons

Discovered by Galileo Galilei in 1610, these four largest satellites are intriguing miniature planetary worlds of their own.

IO

Volcanic World

The most geologically active body in the Solar System, hosting over 400 active volcanoes fueled by intense gravitational tidal heating.

Diameter: 3,643 km Orbit: 1.77 Days

EUROPA

Ocean World

Covered by a smooth ice shell, Europa hides a vast liquid ocean underneath containing twice as much water as all Earth oceans combined.

Diameter: 3,122 km Orbit: 3.55 Days

GANYMEDE

Largest Moon

Larger than Mercury, Ganymede is the largest moon in our Solar System and the only moon known to generate its own internal magnetic field.

Diameter: 5,268 km Orbit: 7.15 Days

CALLISTO

Ancient Cratered

Boasts the most heavily cratered surface in the Solar System. Its ancient surface shows virtually no geologic activity for 4 billion years.

Diameter: 4,821 km Orbit: 16.69 Days

Human Exploration Missions

Key robotic spacecraft sent across the Solar System to uncover Jupiter's secrets.

1973

Pioneer 10 & 11

First Flybys & Close Images

Pioneer 10 became the first spacecraft to traverse the asteroid belt and obtain close-up images of Jupiter's intense radiation belts.

1979

Voyager 1 & 2

Ring Discovery & Active Volcanoes

Returned thousands of vivid photos revealing active volcanoes on Io and discovering Jupiter’s faint planetary ring system.

1995 - 2003

Galileo Orbiter & Probe

First Dedicated Orbiter

Dropped an atmospheric probe into Jupiter and orbited for eight years, finding evidence of subsurface oceans on Europa.

2016 - Present

Juno Spacecraft (NASA)

Polar Orbit & Interior Mapping

Peers beneath Jupiter's dense clouds to measure water content, map magnetic fields, and capture stunning high-resolution polar storms.

2023 - 2031+

JUICE & Europa Clipper

Searching for Habitable Moons

ESA's JUICE and NASA's Europa Clipper missions are en route to study Ganymede, Callisto, and Europa for potential habitability.
